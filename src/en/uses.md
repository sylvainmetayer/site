## Development

- **Editor**: [VS Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/).
- **Tool versions**: [mise](https://mise.jdx.dev/), after years of [asdf](https://asdf-vm.com/) (I gave [a talk](https://github.com/sylvainmetayer/talk-asdf) about it).
- **Secrets**: [SOPS](https://github.com/getsops/sops), so nothing is committed in plain text ([talk](https://github.com/sylvainmetayer/talk-sops)).
- **Workstation**: installed and configured as code, so it can be rebuilt identically ([talk](https://github.com/sylvainmetayer/talk-workstation-automation)).
