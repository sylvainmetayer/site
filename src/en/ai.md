How I use AI, in the spirit of [/ai pages](https://slashpages.net/#ai).

## For coding

I use [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) and [Claude](https://claude.ai/), mostly on side projects, to get familiar with agents and try out small developments.

What I am exploring at the moment: handing a whole task to an agent (exploring, coding, reviewing), building my own agents and automating some tasks. I keep the choices and the review.

## On this site

Part of the code of this site is written with Claude Code. I review every change before publishing it, and the CI checks apply as they do for everything else ([under the hood](/en/under-the-hood/)).

The articles and the notes are mine. AI sometimes helps me proofread: spelling, phrasing, broken links.
