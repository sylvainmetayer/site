Why this site exists.

- **Owning my content.** What I write is published here first, on my own domain name. Networks and platforms only get a link or a copy ([how](/en/posse/)).
- **A playground.** This is where I try out web techniques, tools and ideas, with nothing at stake ([under the hood](/en/under-the-hood/)).
- **An entry point.** My [CV](/en/cv/), my [talks](/en/talks/) and my [projects](/en/projects/) are gathered in one place.
