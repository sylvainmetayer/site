The instructions for my relatives if I were to die, in the spirit of [/death pages](https://slashpages.net/#death).

The principle: an encrypted document listing my accounts, what to cancel and the data to keep. Its key is split into several shares, handed to relatives: two of them are needed to open it.

None of this is online.
