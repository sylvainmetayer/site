The major changes to the site. The details are in [the git history]({{ global.repository }}/commits/main/).

## 2026

- **13 November**: /concerts page (in French), fed from setlist.fm, and the [slash pages](/en/slashes/).
- **10 November**: web [business card](/en/card/), with vCard and QR code.
- **8 November**: SocialSpec files (`about.md`, `contact.vcf`, `following.opml`).
- **30 October**: [Opquast](https://www.opquast.com/) audit: privacy, licence, [sitemap](/en/sitemap/), [glossary](/en/glossary/), search.
- **29 October**: tags, talk schedule, [colophon](/en/under-the-hood/), [/uses](/en/uses/), notes and links; series, related articles and interactive table of contents; Shiki highlighting and callouts; sharing to Mastodon and Bluesky, outgoing webmentions, comments; JSON-LD, breadcrumb, short links.
- **5 October**: new "Prompt" design on Eleventy 3, move to Cloudflare Pages, English version of the site, webmentions, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> palette.
- **2 October**: the referral page moves to [ref.sylvain.dev](https://ref.sylvain.dev/).
