The application I open for each need, in the spirit of [/defaults pages](https://defaults.rknight.me/). The details, hardware included, are on [/uses](/en/uses/).

- **System**: Fedora Workstation, with GNOME
- **Browser**: Firefox
- **Passwords**: KeePassXC
- **Notes**: Obsidian
- **Files**: kDrive, and Nextcloud at home
- **Photos**: Immich
- **RSS feeds**: FreshRSS
- **Bookmarks**: Karakeep
- **Films and series**: Jellyfin
- **Series tracking**: BetaSeries
- **Concerts**: setlist.fm
- **Travel**: TREK
- **Groceries and recipes**: KitchenOwl
- **Messaging**: Signal
- **Terminal**: ddterm, with Zellij and Starship
- **Code editor**: VS Code, IntelliJ IDEA at work
