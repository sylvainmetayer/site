On February 1st, I gave my first live meetup talk with onepoint, on YouTube. The topic was "Terraform, REX d'un développeur" (Terraform, a developer's experience report). For those who would like to watch it, the replay is available here: [YouTube link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GR_YJIfo8jg&list=PLm-YYS9h2JhWiAZ5QuuJD6jyghWjkK09q&index=27).

I'm proud of myself, as I usually prefer being behind the camera. Being encouraged to share this experience report pushed me out of my comfort zone, and in the end, the key takeaway is that 45 minutes is both long and short :)
